# Topic vocabulary and sentence bank

Learn vocabulary in phrases, not isolated words. A phrase gives you the gender, the preposition and a ready-made sentence for the oral or written paper.

## False friends and common slips

| Spanish | Meaning | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| actual | current | actual |
| actualmente | currently | actually |
| largo | long | large |
| embarazada | pregnant | embarrassed |
| realizar | to carry out | to realise |
| asistir | to attend | to assist |
| el idioma | language | idiom |
| la librería | bookshop | library |
| el vaso | glass | vase |
| sensible | sensitive | sensible |

Also watch:

- `hacer` = to do/make; `hacer un examen` = to take an exam.
- `tomar` = to take; `tomar una decisión` = to make a decision.
- `tener` + age: `tengo diecisiete años`, not `soy diecisiete`.
- `gustar` agrees with the thing: `me gustan los libros`.

## A daily five-minute routine

Choose one topic. Cover the English column and say five sentences aloud: one opinion, one past example, one future plan, one advantage and one disadvantage. Then write two of them down and check the verbs. Rotate topics across the week.
