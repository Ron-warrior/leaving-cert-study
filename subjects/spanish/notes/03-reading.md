# Reading: Sections A and B

Reading comprehension is the largest part of the written paper. Across Sections A and B it is worth **170 marks** — 120 for comprehension and 50 for the opinion piece that follows the Section B text.

The texts are not meant to be translated word for word. You need to find the answer, understand the attitude behind it and express the answer in the language the question asks for.

## What is in the reading sections?

### Section A — 70 marks

| Question | Marks | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question 1 | 50 | Prescribed literature **or** a journalistic text |
| Question 2 | 20 | Two short comprehensions (a) and (b) |

For Question 1 you choose **one** option. The literature option asks about the prescribed text; the journalistic option asks about a real-world article. Most students choose the journalistic text unless they have studied the literature thoroughly.

### Section B — 100 marks

| Question | Marks | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Questions 1–4 | 50 | Vocabulary, synonyms, explaining phrases and comprehension of a longer text |
| Question 5 | 50 | Opinion piece in Spanish, 80–150 words |

The Section B text is longer and often deals with a social issue: technology, health, environment, education, tourism, work or culture. Recent examples include a 2026 text asking whether learning a second language is worth it, and a 2025 text about AI influencers. The comprehension questions may ask you to:

- find a word or phrase in the text that means the same as a given word
- explain a phrase **in English** as it is used in the text
- answer a question in Spanish or English, following the instruction exactly
- summarise a paragraph or identify the writer's attitude
- distinguish fact from opinion

## Recent paper themes

These are the topics the 2024, 2025 and 2026 Higher Level papers actually used. They are useful vocabulary practice, not a prediction.

| Year | Section A | Section B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | *El medallón perdido* (literature) or a cruise-ship lifestyle article; a potato-omelette debate and Isabel Allende | Is learning a second language worth it? |
| 2025 | *Relato de un náufrago* (literature) or Spanish customs article; an accessible city and padel in Ireland | AI influencers |
| 2024 | *Relato de un náufrago* (literature) or the rise of Spanish-language music; parking with a fake disabled badge and Cuenca as a food capital | The Buenos Aires hurling club |

Notice the mix: culture, tourism, food, sport, technology, education and personal experience. Build vocabulary across those domains rather than guessing one topic.

## The reading method

1. **Read the title and the first paragraph.** Establish the topic, the writer's attitude and the text type.
2. **Read the questions before the detailed reading.** Underline the key words and the line references.
3. **Scan for the answer.** Do not read every sentence at the same speed. Move between the question and the relevant paragraph.
4. **Answer in the required language.** If the question says "in English", an answer in Spanish may not be accepted. If it says "en español", answer in Spanish.
5. **Use the line references.** They are there to stop you searching the whole text and to settle ambiguities.
6. **Keep answers short and precise.** A comprehension answer usually needs the relevant detail, not a full translation.
7. **Move on.** If a question is taking too long, mark it and return after you have collected the easier marks.

## Synonyms and vocabulary questions

A synonym question asks you to find a word or phrase in the text with a similar meaning. The answer must come **from the text**, not from your own vocabulary.

Example:

> Find a word in lines 5–10 that means *contaminación*.

If the text says `la polución del aire empeora cada año`, the answer is `polución`, not `contaminación`.

### Common synonym pairs

| Given word | Possible text word |
| --- | --- |
| importante | fundamental, clave, esencial |
| problema | dificultad, obstáculo, reto |
| mejorar | avanzar, progresar, perfeccionar |
| aumentar | crecer, subir, incrementarse |
| reducir | disminuir, bajar, limitar |
| mucha gente | la mayoría, millones de personas |
| ventaja | beneficio, punto a favor |
| desventaja | inconveniente, punto en contra |
| peligroso | arriesgado, perjudicial |
| útil | práctico, beneficioso, eficaz |
| cansado | agotado, fatigado |
| contento | satisfecho, feliz, encantado |

Do not choose a word just because it looks similar. Check that it fits the grammar and meaning of the sentence.

## Explaining a phrase in English

This question tests whether you understand the phrase **in context**, not whether you can translate every word.

Example:

> Explain in English: `la situación está fuera de control`.

A weak answer is "the situation is outside of control". A strong answer is "the situation can no longer be managed".

### Method

1. Find the phrase in the text and read the whole sentence.
2. Identify what the phrase refers to: a person, a trend, a problem, a result.
3. Explain the meaning in natural English.
4. Do not leave Spanish words in an English explanation unless they are names.

## Summary questions

A summary question asks you to reduce a paragraph or section to its main point. It is not a list of every detail.

- Identify the topic sentence.
- Ask: what is the writer saying about this topic?
- Include the main idea and one or two supporting details.
- Use your own words where possible; do not copy whole sentences.
- Keep within the word limit or line space given.

## True, false and not given

- **True:** the text says the same thing, even if the wording is different.
- **False:** the text says the opposite, or contradicts the statement.
- **Not given:** the information is not stated, even if it seems likely or you know it from outside the text.

The last category is the one students overthink. If the text does not give the information, do not infer it from general knowledge.

## The Section B opinion piece

Question 5 is worth **50 marks**: **25 for content/communication** and **25 for language**. You write **80–150 words in Spanish** on one of three statements.

A good opinion piece has:

- a clear introduction that states your position
- two developed reasons, each with an example or consequence
- a counterpoint or qualification (optional but impressive)
- a short conclusion
- connectors that show the shape of the argument

### Structure

| Part | Length | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | 1–2 sentences | State the topic and your opinion |
| Reason 1 | 2–3 sentences | Explain and give an example |
| Reason 2 | 2–3 sentences | Add a different reason or consequence |
| Counterpoint | 1–2 sentences | Acknowledge the other side |
| Conclusion | 1–2 sentences | Restate your position |

### Useful opinion language

| Function | Spanish |
| --- | --- |
| In my opinion | `En mi opinión`, `A mi parecer` |
| I think that | `Creo que`, `Pienso que`, `Me parece que` |
| I agree | `Estoy de acuerdo con` |
| I disagree | `No estoy de acuerdo con` |
| On the one hand | `Por un lado` |
| On the other hand | `Por otro lado` |
| However | `Sin embargo`, `No obstante` |
| For example | `Por ejemplo` |
| Because | `porque`, `ya que`, `debido a que` |
| Therefore | `por lo tanto`, `así que` |
| In conclusion | `En conclusión`, `Para terminar` |

### Keep the language controlled

The opinion piece is not the place to attempt a complicated subjunctive you have not mastered. Clear present, past and future sentences with good agreement will score better than ambitious grammar that breaks down. Include one or two complex structures you can use accurately: `si + imperfect subjunctive` (e.g. `si tuviéramos más tiempo`), `es importante que + subjunctive`, or a conditional.

## A 15-minute reading check

After a practice paper, list every lost mark under one heading:

- I could not find the answer.
- I found it but answered in the wrong language.
- I understood it but wrote too much or too little.
- I did not know the vocabulary.
- I misread the question.

The pattern tells you what to practise next. Most students lose more marks to question-reading and answer-format errors than to unknown vocabulary.
