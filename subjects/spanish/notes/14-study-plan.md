# The 12-week plan: from notes to exam performance

Spanish rewards regular contact more than last-minute cramming. This plan assumes about **45–60 minutes a day**, six days a week, with one rest day. If you have less time, keep the daily oral and vocabulary blocks and shorten the reading.

## The weekly shape

| Day | Focus | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | New chapter + vocabulary | 45 min |
| Tuesday | Grammar drills + oral recording | 45 min |
| Wednesday | Reading or listening paper | 50 min |
| Thursday | Writing task + correction | 50 min |
| Friday | Oral roleplay + conversation questions | 40 min |
| Saturday | Full timed section or mock | 60–90 min |
| Sunday | Rest or light vocabulary review | 15 min |

Do not count passive reading as study. Every session should produce something you can check: a written answer, a recording, a score or a corrected error.

## Weeks 1–3: build the toolkit

### Week 1 — oral and tenses

- Read **The oral** and **Grammar** chapters.
- Record 60-second answers to five personal questions.
- Learn the present, preterite and future forms of ten high-frequency verbs.
- Write five sentences about last weekend and five about next year.

**Check:** can you talk about yourself for two minutes without reading?

### Week 2 — reading and vocabulary

- Read **Reading** and **Vocabulary**.
- Do one Section A comprehension and one Section B text under time.
- Build a 40-word topic sheet for family, school and free time.
- Learn ten connectors and use five in a paragraph.

**Check:** can you find an answer and write it in the required language without copying the whole sentence?

### Week 3 — writing formats

- Read **Writing** and **Model answers**.
- Write one opinion piece, one formal letter and one diary entry.
- Correct them with the checklist, then rewrite the weakest one.
- Learn the five-point structure for dialogue and roleplay.

**Check:** do you know the format and mark split for every Section C task?

## Weeks 4–6: practise under pressure

### Week 4 — listening

- Do the 2024 listening paper in full, then the 2025 paper later in the week.
- Do not read the transcript until you have checked your answers.
- Re-listen to each section with the transcript and underline the exact answer words.
- Learn ten weather, travel and school phrases from the audio.

**Check:** can you get the gist on the first hearing and the detail on the second?

### Week 5 — reading speed

- Do a full Section A and Section B reading paper in 65 minutes.
- Mark every lost mark by cause: not found, wrong language, too long, vocabulary, misread question.
- Learn the synonym pairs in the Reading chapter.
- Do the four anthology texts in this pack.

**Check:** are you losing marks because of comprehension or because of answer format?

### Week 6 — oral performance

- Use the **Oral trainer**: two roleplays and eight conversation questions.
- Record yourself and listen for pauses, verb endings and pronunciation.
- Do a mock oral with a teacher, friend or voice recording.
- Prepare three roleplays from the current SEC list; do not rely only on this pack's practice cards.

**Check:** can you keep a conversation going for ten minutes and cover all five roleplay points?

## Weeks 7–9: full papers and targeted repair

### Week 7 — full written paper

- Sit a full 2-hour 30-minute paper in one block.
- Use the timing plan: Section A 50 minutes, Section B 55 minutes, Section C 35 minutes, 10 minutes checking.
- Mark it against the scheme and list the top three error types.

### Week 8 — repair week

- Reread the grammar sections behind your top three errors.
- Do the matching drills in the grammar workbook.
- Rewrite one opinion piece and one Section C task with those errors fixed.
- Do one listening section and one reading text every day.

### Week 9 — second full paper

- Sit another full paper, ideally a different year.
- Compare the score and the error list with Week 7.
- If a section is still weak, spend the following week on that section only.

## Weeks 10–11: exam simulation

### Week 10 — timed sections

| Day | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Section A, 50 minutes |
| Tuesday | Section B reading, 35 minutes |
| Wednesday | Opinion piece, 30 minutes |
| Thursday | Section C, 35 minutes |
| Friday | Listening paper, 40 minutes |
| Saturday | Oral mock, 15 minutes |

Mark each one immediately and correct the errors the same day.

### Week 11 — full mock and oral

- Do a complete written paper under exam conditions.
- Do a complete oral mock, including the roleplay.
- Review the error log; make a one-page list of your five most frequent mistakes.
- Do not learn new grammar this week. Consolidate what you have.

## Week 12: taper

- **Monday:** light vocabulary and one oral recording.
- **Tuesday:** one reading text and one listening section.
- **Wednesday:** one opinion piece and one Section C task; no new material.
- **Thursday:** review the error log and model answers.
- **Friday:** 20-minute oral warm-up; rest.
- **Saturday:** light review only.
- **Sunday:** rest.

The night before the exam, do not cram a new chapter. Review your sentence bank, your five recurring errors and the timing plan.

## Score targets

Marks are out of 400. A realistic target is built from the components, not from a vague “H1” hope.

| Component | Marks | Strong target | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oral | 100 | 80+ | Conversation fluency and all roleplay points |
| Aural | 80 | 60+ | Detail, numbers and question-matching |
| Section A | 70 | 55+ | Comprehension and answer format |
| Section B | 100 | 75+ | Reading plus a clear opinion piece |
| Section C | 50 | 38+ | Format, five points, correct verbs |
| **Total** | **400** | **308+** | Roughly an H2/H1 boundary depending on the year |

These are practice targets, not grade boundaries. The SEC sets grade bands each year.

## The error log

Keep one page with four columns:

| Date | My sentence | Correction | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 May | `Nosotros habla español.` | `Nosotros hablamos español.` | -ar, nosotros = -amos |
| 13 May | `Me gusto el cine.` | `Me gusta el cine.` | gustar agrees with the thing |

Review the log for five minutes before every practice paper. The same mistake should not appear three times.

## Exam-day timing

### Written paper — 2 hours 30 minutes

| Time | Task |
| --- | --- |
| 0–50 | Section A: Question 1 and Question 2 |
| 50–85 | Section B: Questions 1–4 |
| 85–115 | Section B: Question 5 opinion piece |
| 115–135 | Section C: Question 1 |
| 135–145 | Section C: Question 2 |
| 145–150 | Check verbs, accents, word count and required points |

If you run over, protect the opinion piece and Section C. They are worth 100 marks between them and reward a clear structure.

### Oral

- Arrive early and warm up with five sentences aloud.
- Greet the examiner politely: `Buenos días, ¿cómo está?`
- If you do not understand, ask for repetition rather than guessing silently.
- Cover every roleplay point; one missing point can cost six marks.
- Finish with a clear close: `Muchas gracias por su tiempo.`

## The one-page revision sheet

By the last week, condense your notes to one page:

- five opinion openers
- five connectors
- three tense patterns
- five roleplay phrases
- five topic vocabulary items
- your five recurring errors

If you can explain that page aloud, you are ready.
